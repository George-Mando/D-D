🌐 PARTY STORY SYNC HOW IT WORKS
v4.6 STORY SYNC: When any player sends an action, the DM response is broadcast to ALL party members at the same time via MQTT. Everyone sees and hears the exact same story simultaneously — just like a real D&D table.

A green 📡 STORY SYNC LIVE banner confirms all players are receiving the same narration. Roll results are also shared so everyone sees your dice roll.
